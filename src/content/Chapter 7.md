### The Temporal Jungle

Salem jerked back at the sight of the gatling gun. He had never seen such a blaster, nor the ghouls that were loaded inside. The scarlet vials with black trimmings spoke of evil intent, Ravik radiating with a contradictory heroic aura. He was changed.

“Ravik…What happened?” Salem shouted, trying to calm the now ghouled beast. Ravik wasn't himself anymore. Something had infected him deeply.

Ravik sighed, then took out an Aquafreak onto his palms. “Do you see this? At the bottom of the serpent's den, this Dark Water has shown me…The Jungle grows restless…We can finally reach our potential…”

Iris's expression grew terrified at the sound of the word “dark water”, scrambling backwards. Her fingers dug into the soft soil, her fists slowly clenching hard scooping up the dirt. Salem circled around the garden bed, making sure Ravik didn't make a move on Iris. Salem knew his duty well, but he couldn't just leave Iris. He needed to protect Iris. She was the only way Salem could learn more of the world.

“You know?\! The Lawa keep saying they are expanding, but they are clearly not. They need my help. They *trust* me.” Ravik explained calmly. Iris could see the connection. This was it, the fate of the jungle resting in her and Salem's hands.

“You'll never win\! What you're doing— this ghouling of slugs— it'll be the end of all of Slugterra\!” Iris cried. Ravik swung a quick glance, but ultimately ignored Iris's cry, whistling almost for something to come to assist him. At the sound of Ravik's piercing whistle, a loud rumbling shook the ground as his mechabeast bolted into view. A sleek, red trimmed mecha beast that depicted that of a jaguar stood boldly under the Solaryte crystals. Ravik saddled on, smirking at Iris. He could see fear tremble in her eyes.

“Salem\! This is bad\! We can't let him break the wall\!” Iris exclaimed, tugging on Salem to do something as Ravik sped off. All there was left was a faint crimson streak diffusing into the air, giving Iris and Salem room to pursue.

“Alright then, you drive\! I have an idea\!” Salem replied, calling his mechabeast in. As the two saddled together, Iris took the wheel while Salem equipped his sniper rifle, ready to bullet down Ravik. 

The fight ensued at the mouth of the dense canopy of trees where Ravik had a significant lead. Ravik knew the area from the back of his hand. He already knew where he was going to go. As they tightly pursued Ravik on their mechs through the overgrown parts of the jungle, Salem carefully chose his Slicksilver and loaded it into his blaster. Salem's sniper combusted a loud whirring sound, charging up the fusion core. The accelerator flickered orange, reaching a state of radiance. A small spray of warm, yellow gas filled the vial, infusing with the Slicksilver.

“What's that sound?” Iris asked, glancing at Salem's sniper.

Salem, steady and focused, didn't mutter a single word. Then, Salem pulled the trigger, firing the Slicksilver. The Slicksilver immediately transformed, with no delay or wind up time whatsoever, surprising Iris. “It's called flow state,”

The spikes of the Slicksilver unsheathed. “It's a chemical injection only sniper rifles can produce,”

The faint reflection of leaves glinted along its geometric scales. It dashed forwards, leaving nothing but a thin streak of silver fading into the air. Iris couldn't even see it, the Slicksilver was already beside Ravik. “Slugs in this state travel extremely fast, but this reaction slows their perception of time,”

“It helps them to have more accurate and precise movements while airborne\!" Salem explained, pointing towards his Slicksilver up ahead. Its metallic shell stuck its magnetic tail out, gravitating all metallic objects within its radius to its toughened tail. It violently pulled Ravik’s mechabeast and gatling gun onto it, disarming him and leaving him vulnerable. Ravik collapsed forward, but the Vexlet that stood upon his shoulder didn’t. The Vexlet in fact sneered straight at the Slicksilver who couldn't resist but to laugh at it, poking its tongue out. But the Slicksilver played right into the mischievous Vexlet's trap. The Slicksilver, not paying attention crashed into a tree, forcing it back into protoform where it fell onto the ground with a heavy thud, knocking it out. Ravik's mechabeast violently tumbled down but fortunately in one piece while his quick reactions gracefully caught his gatling gun in his hands. Salem's eyes widened, shocked at how Ravik had survived his Slicksilver. 

The chase continued, but Ravik wasn't ahead as much as before. The distance between Salem and Ravik had reduced significantly and Iris could now see every action Ravik made. As they turned the corner towards the wall, three more mechabeasts joined pursuit. 

“Who's that Salem?” the voice boomed. Salem grew tense as he stared at the head of hunting, Aleku. Aleku's eyes squinted, inspecting Iris. Iris didn't dare to look, focusing on the path ahead. “Is that…a foreigner?”

Salem kept silent, but before he could react, he noticed Aleku also held a crimson red slug with dark patches of grey embroidered onto its body. Salem was certain that it was a ghoul. “Master…You have…Ghouls too?”

“So what? Ravik tells us we can break the wall with this. This is big. What you are doing is only slowing our progress down,” Aleku snarled, flexing his Barreto slug. “Consider this your punishment…”

“Iris, look out\!” Salem yelled, trying to steer the mech in a different direction. Aleku was out for punishment. Aleku gracefully tossed the slug shell upwards into the air, perfectly catching it with his blaster. The shell snapped in place with a satisfying click, followed by the sudden whirr of the accelerator to hasten the energy within the blaster's core. The Barreto was launched no more than a second later, transforming and wrapping its stretchy, rope-like body around the legs of Salem's mech. The mech suddenly braking with the applied force launched Iris and Salem both off, causing them to crash and tumble on the uneven gravel path. 

“Ouch…” Iris groaned, trying to get up. A firm grip grappled around her neck, pulling her up and in front of Aleku's face.

“Tell me traveler, where do you come from?” Aleku demanded, holding his blaster firmly in his other hand. Iris squirmed like a worm, trying to escape his iron grip. Iris didn't want to speak.

“Iris…don't tell them…a thing…*yet*…” Salem mumbled, still laying on the ground. 

“Pathetic. Ravik, you said you wanted to demonstrate how we are going to break the wall?” the tribe leader motioned. Ravik smiled, equipping the gatling gun loaded with Aquafreaks.

Salem, still down raised his hand, desperately trying to get Ravik's attention. “No…don't…”

Of course, nobody paid attention to Salem. Only the sound of more trotting approached, ringing from ear to ear. Reinforcement was on its way as carriage mechs galloped into sound. Guards rushed over surveying Iris and Salem's statuses, overseeing their every move.

“Ready?” Ravik signalled, commencing his show. As he unleashed the barrage of the deathly Aquafreaks, Salem shouted like background noise— loud but ignored. Their water jets concentrated onto one spot, slowly cutting and eroding away the wall. A deep hole appeared as the Aquafreaks’ dark water contaminated the wall, infecting its evil nature to hasten the wreckage. The hole wasn't deep, it was minimal, but it was progress. More than what the Lawa tribe had done in 5 years. If anyone was to finally help the Lawa to turn into a thriving tribe, Ravik knew it was him.

“What do you think, Lucian?” Ravik smirked as he gazed at their leader. Their tribe leader Lucian was immediately impressed, stroking his chin in excitement.

“Lucian No\!” Salem yelled weakly, trying to stand up. “You can't— you don't know what's beyond the wall\!”

“And you do?” Lucian snapped, expecting Salem to answer through his struggles. The guards restrained Salem back down onto the ground, using their feet to lock him in place. Salem remained silent, not wanting to spoil Iris's secrets. 

“You'll— No, *we'll* all die,” Salem muttered quietly, hoping Lucian didn't hear his voice. 

“No matter. Back to Lahos Town men\!” Lucian commanded. His guards saddled Salem into a carriage mech. The seat reeked of sweat, stained with blood. The rope around his hands was bound tight, digging into his skin. Red marks were already appearing as the carriage rattled against the gravel path, finding its way back to the main town.

It was before the Solaryte crystals had completely dimmed overhead that the carriage arrived back to Lahos Town, the bustling city centre for the Temporal Jungle. It wasn't considered late yet— the cool triad of colours painted the quiet town at dusk, where the main hall was lit by a warm and daring yellow hue.

The hall was crowded by trolls and humans, all awaiting for the special announcement that Chief Lucian had planned. Eyes darted around eagerly like fishes out of water, glancing and discussing with each other before their eyes finally locked onto the stage where Lucian appeared behind the podium. “Dear members of the Lawa clan, we are pleased to announce we have finally found a method to expand our nation. Welcome our old fisherman friend, Ravik Tohra\!”

The tribe leader smiled as Ravik presented the ghoul. “These ghouls as Ravik per says are able to chip away against the wall. Say, you have more?”

Ravik nodded a yes, nodding slowly in agreement. Salem eyed Lucian from the back of the room, fueled with anger and hatred. His hand bindings had loosened from the carriage ride, the bumps slowly nudging the knot apart. The guard beside him took no notice, concentrating on their leader's speech.

“Then it's settled\! We'll begin proper expansion tomorrow. You can be in charge of the wall management then,” the tribe leader exclaimed, praising Ravik for his work.

Salem burst out, unable to resist his anger. He barged through the crowd, exhausted and trying to catch his breath. Sweat trickled down every inch of his body as he lunged towards Lucian, but Ravik was prepared. He swiftly slung a Briardrill, where its spiky vines grappled onto Salem's body, pulling him down and trapping him. Salem fell to his knees and his hands were quickly entangled by the Briardrill's vines, presenting him right in front of their chief, Lucian. His teeth gritted forcefully as he tried to hold his anger and frustration back. Nobody would believe him. A desert? Nobody could guess such a shift in terrain was waiting on the other side of the wall, ready to consume the bountiful jungle. 

“No…Lucian…” Salem mumbled. The spikes on the vines dug deeper into his skin. The prickling pierced small wounds that began to seep strands of blood out, pooling on the hard wood floor below. “You can't…”

“Silence\! Do you see what Ravik has done? This *thing* is the key to our prosperity\! He's ridden the damned Hexlet, and now with this power, we'll be showered in land\!” Lucian exclaimed, praising Ravik and his ghouls. Nothing went right for him, everything almost folded upon him like a lengthy game of misfortune. “Let this be a demonstration of our new found power\! We will prosper\!”

Salem had no choice. The ropes bit into his skin, the Briardrill’s thorns still lingering in shallow cuts across his arms. Around him, the hall roared with approval. Voices blended into a deafening chorus that drowned out reason. Ravik stood at the centre of it all, not as a traitor, but as a saviour in their eyes. Salem lowered his head. There was no fighting this. Not here. Not like this. If the wall fell, the jungle would follow. And there was nothing he could do to stop it.

The night settled quietly over Lahos Town. The celebration had long faded into scattered chatter, the village dimming under the soft glow of the Solaryte crystals. Guards moved in routine patterns, their footsteps dull against the hardened paths, watchful—but unworried. To them, the future had already been secured.

In the distance, not far from the hall was a hut that still remained bright at the dead of night. The small flicker of light burned late, housing the body. The ropes hung Iris ritually against the wall in the small hut. Her blaster was disarmed, placed far away out of reach. The hut seemed to be a strange room filled with vials of some serum, almost like a storage room that wasn't supposed to house her.

“Hello?” Iris winced, checking if anyone was around. No sound. She was alone. The room was awfully silent and dramatic, with the singular light source diffusing a warm but ominous highlight against the darkened surfaces of the hut. It didn't feel that lost as she noticed her slugs hopping out from beneath the shadows, coming into view. They weren't harmed— but Iris, she was still pinned to the wall. Her Makobreaker jumped energetically, chewing through the toughened rope with its razor-like teeth, freeing her. 

It was time to escape. Iris knew she couldn't stand against the Jungle by herself. She needed help. As she pushed the door open, the silhouette of the cave troll appeared in front of her. Iris dazed, stood frozen, muttering in terror. “Ravik? What are you—” 

“Tell me…Where are you from?”

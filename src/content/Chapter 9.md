### The Temporal Jungle, Part 3

**12 Years Ago…**

Salem pointed his wooden toy towards Lucian, the newest tribe leader after the recent passing of his father, the previous tribe leader Lucius. He exercised wildly as he jumped around, playing amongst the trees. He felt free at heart, filled with nothing but joy and peace. The jungle was the perfect place to live. 

“Aren't you a strong boy?” Lucian smiled, playing with Salem. Lucian's smile, it was rather dazzling in a sense, his hair bathing under the Solaryte crystals as he acted elegantly. 

“I'm going to be the greatest hunter of all Lawas\!” Salem exclaimed cheerfully, trying to mimic the bold slugslingers that slung the cute but fatally strong slugs across the air. “This is Chompy, my Tenasher\!”

Lucian grinned at Salem, turning his attention back to his induction. It was already. Suddenly, Salem interrupted Lucian's attention. “When can I be a hunter, Lucian? When can I get my blaster?”

Salem's voice was optimistic and cheerful, laced with an eager undertone that surprised him. Lucian stopped cold for a moment, his words hesitant to come out. There was a strange sour tone to his words, and it felt bitter to hear his pain as he spoke. “Uh? I'm not too sure. This wall that surrounds us…We want to expand because we're slowing down significantly on technology…But…We don't know what's on the other side… Sorry, I need to prepare for the feast. It looks like Ravik is back…”

\~

That was all Salem could think about as his hands were cuffed tightly by metallic chains that bound him against the wall. Lucian stared into Salem's soul like a demon, without any sense of forgiveness.

“Lucian…That day…You just disappeared…” Salem mumbled weakly. He kicked towards his back pocket, where his Tenasher had hid, out of sight, hiding from Lucian's capture. “The feast…it was all ruined…I can now understand the lies…The deception…The *fear* within you…”

“Please, I'm changed,” Lucian snapped. “I've overcome my fear of what's beyond the wall, there's nothing we can't beat. Not anymore, at least.”

Salem's anger tensed. His voice boomed like a desperate child. “Tell me what happened after you left me…I still remember that day clearly even as of right now\!” 

Lucian ignored Salem's yells and calmly walked out the room. Salem’s fists clenched. The moment Lucian had left Salem's view, he scrambled his chains off. His Tenasher, scales tainted pale red, had violently gnawed through them, freeing Salem from the prison. All he had to do was confront Lucian. To Salem, there was no other choice. As he moved through the rooms of the complex, Salem snatched a blaster off a desk, with his Tenasher being his only ammunition. His rifle and arsenal of slugs were secured tightly at a remote location that he had no idea about. It was truly him against the world. The halls felt deafeningly quiet as his footsteps trotted quietly against the wooden floors. 

“Lucian is gone. I'll tell you what happened. It's ugly, kid,” Aleku snorted, slapping Salem firmly on the back. He had appeared out of nowhere, catching Salem off guard. It was almost like he was overwatching the entire situation. His grip held firm as his other blaster pointed a Briardrill towards his neck, ready to quickly strangle Salem if he made a move. “You see, Ravik discovered the existence of a substance beneath the Serpent's den that day…When the three of us dove down, something changed.”

Salem fought back, twisting Aleku's grip and jousting him away. Aleku's finger automatically pressed down upon the trigger, launching the Briardrill. Its vines immediately untangled, ready to grapple onto Salem, but his own actions were also hastened. Salem pulled the trigger on the prototype blaster, unknowingly also unloading the serum into the capsule. This time, the gas wasn't yellow, it was orange, as if the serum was more concentrated. It felt excessive, almost an overflow. It reeked a foul stench, diluted with some kind of sweetened chemical, much like the original serum Salem knew.

“No\!” Salem yelled as his Tenasher launched out, its body turning a scarlet red, its rainbow scales losing its vibrance. It had become something terrifying, its jaws snapping the Briardrill's vines instantaneously and crushing its body. The vines sprawled uncontrollably, trapping Aleku in the crossfire. Strands loosened in place as Salem fell backwards himself, hitting the cold wood floor. Aleku wasn't distressed, but for some strange reason laughed, noticing Salem's distress. “No… Don't tell me…”

The air wasn’t the same anymore. It was heavy, stinging like venom as Salem watched his Tenasher hop back into protoform, scarlet red with streaks of maroon along its scales. Its eyes, replaced by a neon pink glow and its teeth, now shining a reflective silver. The light within his Tenasher shuddered. It stood daunting, an ebony aura radiating from its power. The Briardrill curled up in fear as the Menasher overpowered it, swaggering its vampire-like teeth. It felt like an eternal flame had erupted from its eyes, burning a longing pain that would heal Salem’s wounds. But it wasn’t. It was everything that Salem didn’t want. Salem’s heart tore apart and then crushed itself, much like a scrap piece of paper being scrumpled into a ball. “No……”

“The serum always has had this substance,” Aleku grunted, grinning widely. “What a lovely Menasher you have.”

There was no shouting. Salem was paralysed like a statue on all fours, watching the horror unfold. The silence was predatory. It was only Aleku’s occasional cheer as Salem watched his Tenasher growl proactively at everything around it. Salem watched the eyes of his slug that had once held reflections of memories recode themselves. The warmth had changed, repurposed into a high-voltage chill. It was the look of a predator realising the cage door had been left unlatched. But deep down, Salem knew there had to be a way to cure this. Whatever this evil substance was, it was not going to stop him. As Salem warmly opened his palm, trying to reclaim his slug, the Menasher snapped, its jaw almost biting off Salem’s finger. He flinched, terrified as Aleku snickered. 

*“Calm down…”* Salem thought to himself as he regained focus. Without hesitating anymore, Salem knew what he had to do, snatching his now ghouled Tenasher, slamming it into his slug shell before bolting outside the complex. He had to escape. It couldn't go on for longer. Aleku pursued closely as he finally threw off the vines, chasing Salem to the lot where their mechs had been stored.

“You can’t outrun me…You can’t outrun your people\! Kahue, this is what you were meant to be\!” Aleku roared, equipping another Briardrill into his blaster. Salem’s stomach gurgled in pain as butterflies flooded inside. His grip tensed, unable to accept that his favourite slug that he grew up with had been corrupted, changed. He couldn’t believe anything.

The sudden shriek of the Briardrill launching roared through the sky. It didn’t throw Salem off. He couldn’t fight back. If anything, firing meant that it would only make his Tenasher more violent, more sinister. Salem had no choice. His Tenasher roared with anger, biting against its shell. His heartbeat spiked, adrenaline rushing and sweat overflowing. Escape was all that was on his mind. He only knew one route, and that was where he first met Iris. The temple. It was rather forbidden to enter, especially with Aleku on his trail, but Salem knew this was his only way to escape, and potentially see the world from a different perspective. His mech scrambled unsteadily as he raced deep into the Temple of the Lawa, running from the raging Aleku. His face fumed with fury, his energy ignited like no other flame, pursuing Salem. His teeth gritted like hungry dogs, ready to tear Salem down for his betrayal to his people. Why would the Lawa disrespect their elders and leaders’ commands? That was all Aleku could think of. 

The tunnels split as Salem recalled the dark encounter with Iris. The labyrinth section was incoming so Salem listened acutely at the environment, observing every whisper and sound. The tense vibrations softened while the echoed drips of water began to flow into audio, falling drop by drop.

*“Straight, left, right, straight”*

Salem recited calmly in his mind as he shut off his mech's high beams, relying on his muscle memory to find the desert. The cool zephyrs the tunnel carried, swayed by his mech drifted along his hair. Salem could feel the path out, the warmth of the breeze already scorching his scalp. Salem's trail had vanished just like that, engulfed by the darkness. Aleku was lost. No light, no nothing. Only his lights faintly shined the way forward, but it wasn’t enough to determine which path Salem had taken. He had escaped. The sacred temple was now trampled, with the wrath of a new generation disrupting their ancestors’ spirits. The chase was reckoning, leaving the formed debris as a signal of chaos within the jungle. Aleku sneered in anger, disappointed at himself for not being able to catch Salem.

*“Traitor…”*

As Salem found the Glowbyss tunnel, Salem knew he was close to getting out, almost beyond the wall. He glanced behind him to ensure that he had removed Aleku off his trail. There was nothing but darkness and a shimmer of water. 

The rays of light cut through the temple like needles, concentrating heat upon his mech and body. He had escaped. As Salem exited what now was known as The Ducts, the immense heat from the desert scorched his tired expression into a dehydrated mess. Salem almost collapsed forwards on his mech, needing water and feeling the exhaustion. But it wasn’t over. The sand nearby had begun to twirl and dance like a tornado, where something faint felt ominously approaching. As Salem gazed through the sandy mist, trying to see what was there, his eyes finally landed on the pair of red eyes staring down at him. 

*“Iris…Is that you?”*

### Rainbow Serpents

“Is this heaven?” Iris asked, light headed as she finally regained her balance. The vibrance sparked through her sandy expression, beaming with an iridescent light that shone off her golden jacket. It felt real, yet so fake to see green. Was this the burning world? No, it couldn't be. The sound of rain crept beneath her ear drums as she finally sat upright upon the stone she had laid on. The boy stood quietly, carrying his rifle on his back as he gazed off into the jungle. 

Silence.

No words were spoken for a minute, only the moist, scented breeze swept across harmonising with the quiet chirps of the local fauna. The leaves rustled in tune as Iris caught her breath, courageously stepping forward towards the boy.

“Hello?” Iris asked. “Where am I?”

The arid blur of the desert haze and heat had slowly translated to a mystical, fragmented field of view of rainbow frosted bokeh flakes. The change was so sudden and jarring that she could not adapt, forcing her to weakly approach the boy. 

“My name is Salem Kahue, sorry if I startled you back in the Temple of the Lawa before,” Salem explained. He sighed without tension, grappling onto a sense of confusion. “Who are you? I haven't seen you around here before…”

“You're answering my question with a question, that's not how it works,” Iris grunted, leaning against the wooden fences guarding the temple. 

“Well, I'm surprised because the only place down here in Slugterra *is* the jungle. I don't know how you can possibly come from anywhere else. What were you even doing in the Temple anyway?” Salem asked, acting smart. He knew that beyond the wall, there was nothing and there was no way of passing it. He knew that because about a decade ago, a group tried to mine through the wall but to no avail. 

“Wait, so there's *more* beyond the wall…” Iris calculated. “Because I'm from the desert…I entered from *The Ducts*, and now I'm here…”

“What desert? Is that something beyond the wall?” Salem questioned. His eyes lit up, his spectacles glimmering with curiosity. He turned his attention towards Iris who gazed innocently into his deep navy eyes and light silver hair. Salem kept direct eye contact with Iris, expecting her to answer his question.

Iris, who was confused, frowned. “And the Jungle is *also* beyond the wall?”

“From what you've told me, it seems that on one side of the wall, there's a desert and a jungle on the other,” Salem concluded, motioning his confusion to Iris with a simple shrug. Iris was as confused as Salem, sighing in disbelief. 

“The Drunken Desert... I was just in a mine, then the ceiling gave way, and now there's... green. Everywhere.” Iris clarified. 

“You probably want to go back home right?” Salem asked, pointing towards the entrance of the temple. “It's a path beyond the wall…” 

“It's not that simple…” Iris sighed, unable to answer Salem's question without the mention of her father. It felt unreal that perhaps her father wasn't dead, perhaps trapped within the jungle when he decided to explore the Boundless Barrier Wall for the truth. “My dad might have come here when trying to learn more about the wall…”

“If you're going to stay here, stay beside me. You know nothing about the jungle. It's deadly, and us, the Lawa tribe do *not* take outsiders as friendly,” Salem expressed. He tugged on his jacket, reforming his unforgiving expression as he began walking down the gravel trail. “Follow me,”

“What? Why would I follow you? How do I know I can trust you?” Iris shouted, motioning for him to slow down. Salem reluctantly paused, and turned towards Iris with a lazy expression.

“Who do you think saved you and brought you here from those tunnels down in that mine or temple?” Salem snapped. Iris could see his frustration and finally understood his intentions. “You should be glad I was nice enough to help you out…Who knows what other creatures lurk down there…You would've been slug food\!”

The prickly tension between them finally snapped, leaving a wary but mutual understanding. Iris's expression grew neutral, realising the essence of understanding and acceptance that she was now in foreign land. She didn't know anything about the jungle. She was like a fish out of water.

“Just follow me,” Salem signalled, leading Iris down a narrow winding path. As they walked, the sound of rushing water grew louder, echoing through the dense canopy. The exotic greenery blushed from the gentle footsteps upon fragile branches. Iris took moments to embrace the vivid scenery the jungle had, much nicer than the arid and barren desert with what it had to offer.

“Where are we going?” Iris asked, interrupting a long pool of silence. Salem paused and sat down, motioning towards his metallic belt.

“I'm a part of a hunting division, and I was called to complete a task. I can't risk you being found by the others. Like I said, us Lawa aren't extremely friendly to outsiders, especially if they find out you're from *beyond* the wall,” Salem expressed, shrugging in disappointment. “So you're stuck with me. We're heading to Rainbow Falls Cavern, one of the jungle's most famous spots.”

“That…sounds legendary…” Iris commented, her eyes lighting up. “What's there? Waterfalls and rainbows?”

Salem smirked, shrugging and acting as if he didn't know. “Well, legend says, there's a Rainbow Serpent that lives in the lake there that is supposed to be our god of fertility. It provides us with water and harvest twice a year\!”

“A Rainbow Serpent? That sounds so awesome\!” Iris exclaimed, listening to Salem's stories in curiosity. Her eagerness grew to journey to the cavern as Salem continued.

“But, that's also the reason I'm going. My chief has reported flooding near the cavern, assuming the serpent has had unusual activity and has been granting excess water and flooding one of our largest farmlands, Jade Garden Cavern. If it's true, *we* need to suppress the serpent,” Salem said sternly, ensuring that his supervision on Iris wasn't just allowing Iris to freely tour the Jungle. 

Iris swallowed the truth hard and accepted Salem's challenge to help him suppress their so-called god. It was a journey worth taking, perhaps a necessary step in finding her father and learning the truth. “Let's go\!” 

Before long, they were nearing Rainbow Falls Cavern, marked by a small wooden sign in front of a tapestry of vines. Iris pushed through a final, heavy curtain of broad-leafed monstera, expecting the cavern ahead to be adorned in a crazy rainbow palette, but instead stepped into a profound, muted ringing silence. Before her, Rainbow Falls Cavern unveiled a hidden sanctuary that seemed to evoke serenity and peace. 

“Woah…” Iris awed in shock as she embraced the cavern's wild landscape. Far in the distance, two massive, weathered stone pillars reached the cavern's ceiling with emerald green moss sticking and growing upon their surfaces. In a small opening on the sides, a small stream appeared that seemed to lead deep into a river, while it raced out of the main jagged aperture like rain forming a majestical waterfall. Iris’s eyes were immediately drawn to them as beautiful, glittering water thundered down from strange holes within the structures into a glossy lake that seemed to house various slugs. It was almost as if the stone pillars were exhaling, gently feeding the lake below with the purity of water.

Balancing the cavern's symmetry stood the central veil holding a much higher cliff that carried a much larger and prominent waterfall that further nourished the lake below. Foam churned and bubbles rose with each step Iris took, blasting her expectations of the jungle to a whole other level. It was hypnotising and so peaceful. The fine, ethereal mist suspended in the air as Iris watched breathless from the ground below up at the ledge of the upper falls. In this stillness, the heavy humidity of the jungle felt transformed into a cool, prismatic breath, and for a moment, Iris felt less like an outsider and more like someone in the tribe in a ritual that blessed one of light and living water. As Iris and Salem ventured further into the cavern near the lake's edge, Iris grinned ear to ear as if she had just entered a whole new world. It was refreshing and much needed.

“This is Rainbow Falls Cavern. A lot of sea slugs gather here. It's a truly mythical space,” Salem explained, pointing towards the wild slugs that happily played near the water. Some drank while others bathed, enjoying the serene environment. Aquabeaks pouted water like fountains while Vinedrills hopped among nearby flora.

Amongst the happy were also a few dozen of slyer slugs. Red and blue fins were also scattered beneath the waters of the Rainbow Falls Cavern, swimming like deadly predators pursuing its next target. Makobreakers swam like sharks while Mesagators lurked like alligators and Tenashers prowled like crocodiles. The three dangerous water slugs, much more powerful than the average Aquabeak, cut around the sandbeds and structures, skulking like predators. Ripples and small currents formed upon the lake’s surface as Salem listened closely to the tune of the Rainbow Serpent. 

Splish, splash.

The ground, covered in a thick residue, was slippery. It was certain something had definitely flooded the cavern. Salem's steps were calm and collected, ensuring that his balance was always sturdy with an almost silent footstep. Iris followed closely also in silence.

“Makobreakers, Tenashers and Mesagators live in these waters,” Salem explained. “Be careful, they bite,”

Blue eyes faded into view as a Mesagator surfaced, taking a deep and ominous gaze before sinking back into the water, invisible. Iris flinched a little bit, terrified of it. Iris instead directed her sights towards the far left side of the central waterfall, inspecting for anything odd. Then she saw it.

“What about that one?” Iris whispered, pointing to a lone Makobreaker sitting on the lake's edge. “Is that…a Makobreaker slug?”

“Yes, indeed. Makobreaker slugs are quite selfish and independent, but I've never seen one seem that sad,” Salem added, gazing towards the saddened slug. 

“I want one\!” Iris whispered again, carefully advancing closer to the Makobreaker.

“Iris–” Salem began, cutting his own sentence off as he slowly understood her determination and compassion in helping slugs. His expression grew positive, grinning at Iris's kind personality as quietly walked beside her, watching her tame the depressed slug.

“Hey buddy, do you want a treat?” Iris crouched down beside the scared slug, offering it a small pellet of slug food. The Makobreaker watched Iris reach down with the brown stud in her hand, nervous on what it should do next. “Don't be scared, I'm only trying to help you\!”

Her voice was cheerful in tone and attitude with high spirits, gently leaning back to signal to the slug that she herself was also slightly afraid. “C'mon now,”

The Makobreaker reluctantly hopped onto her hand and quickly chomped the pellet down, then jumped off her hand again. It chewed for a few seconds before the Makobreaker hopped forward in curiosity, wanting more of the delicious slug food. 

“Aren't you a hungry slug?” Iris smiled, laying down more slug food for the Makobreaker. “Do you want to join my team?” 

Fin, her Hoverbug and her Armashelt popped out of their barrels onto the ground, greeting the Makobreaker. As Iris slid the Makobreaker into a slug shell on her waistband, the lake began to stir, gently shaking as a whirlpool began to form in the centre. It felt like an unnatural force had just arrived from the depths, suddenly piercing out of the glossy water's surface like a sharp needle ripping through paper. The Rainbow Serpent, all majestic in its form, bathed under the Solaryte crystals above. It danced in the lake, stretching its elongated body out. Its scales gleamed bright in all shades of colour, sparkling a blinding array of dazzling colours outward. Iris and Salem watched quietly, admiring the beast's presence at the cavern's entrance. 

“I don't know why it's surfacing…we're not short of water\!…” Salem began, watching the water around the serpent bubble chaotically. White clouds of foam formed formidably as the serpent flailed around even more. “Look at this mess…This excess water will destroy our farmland in the Jade Garden Cavern\!”

Suddenly, the serpent roared, emitting a terrifying screech that was powerful enough to divert the nearby falls into the other direction for a moment before they came all crashing down, giving the Rainbow Serpent a truly dramatic entrance. It further flooded the cavern floor, forcing Iris and Salem to hold on to nearby flora to keep their balance.

“What the hell?” Iris yelled, her voice racing with panic. Salem remained calm, watching her thoughts and stress flow straight out of her mouth and actions. “How do you suppress this thing?”

The Rainbow Serpent flicked its tail, sweeping up a shotgun of slugs out of the water and launching them all to velocimorph. Three jet blue Aquabeak and two intimidating Mesagator slugs appeared in an instant, forming daunting creatures that targeted Salem and Iris.

The Aquabeaks all shot out high pressured water at extraordinary velocities, adding to the already flooded surface of the cavern. As Salem and Iris attempted to dodge the streaks of water, the Mesagators took the chase themselves, diving into the thin layer of water on the ground as a vehicle of stealth. 

“Stay away from the puddles\! Get on the rocks\!” Salem yelled. Iris flinched backwards as a Mesagator swiped upwards, almost biting her leg. The slippery surface of rapid moving water almost unsettled her next steps, her quick reaction time barely saving her from the deadly layer of the thin sheet. “Quickly\! They are deadly slugs that can turn invisible in liquids\!”

Iris frantically scrambled back on balance, and in a panic leapt towards the same stone structure Salem had crouched on. 

“Woah\!” Salem exclaimed, his grip slipping on Iris. “Breathe\! Slow down\!” 

Iris had slipped from her unstable balance and sweat-tampered grip, unable to reach the small rock platform Salem was already on. “Stay there\! Don't move\!”

The lurking Mesagators still present, circled Iris carefully as she sat on the ground silently, terrified at the ghost-like slugs. The ability had already begun to haunt her, and she could not bear it. Iris's Makobreaker then suddenly growled, showing its sharp teeth that quickly revealed and scared the Mesagators back into their protoforms, forcing them to surface and slide back into the lake where they inhabited. Iris finally caught her breath, knowing the Mesagators were gone for now. 

“What happened?” Iris whimpered, still scared of what had just happened.

“This is the jungle…It's *nothing* like a desert,” Salem gritted through his teeth. “Makobreakers are like apex predators to Tenashers and Mesagators, they are fierce and strong…”

Iris innocently nodded, still sat on the ground in an awkward position.

“We can't keep the Rainbow Serpent surfaced… As long as it is awake, it will constantly produce more water\!” Salem described, loading his Tenasher into his sniper. 

“How do we beat it?” Iris questioned, still dodging the occasional Aquabeak jets the serpent flung towards her. Iris's mind was still disturbed by the Mesagators although they had already left the waters below, unable to comprehend their powerful, invisible nature. 

“On my mark, you need to shoot your Makobreaker\!” Salem explained, pointing to the ecstatic slug that hopped within its slug shell on Iris's waistband. Iris hesitated, but her Makobreaker boldly jumped into her blaster on its own, understanding its task.

Iris's actions felt scrambled, her mind unorganised as she fiddled with her blaster while also constantly gazing at the wet surface below, terrified about any new Mesagators the serpent had flung. Her aim was shoddy and uneven, her blaster making micro-adjustments every few seconds. “Uhmm…” 

“Iris\! There's no time\! You have to focus\! If we don't suppress the serpent, the farm will be completely flooded\!” Salem shouted worriedly. Quickly crouching down, Salem leant his rifle upon the rock and held the trigger with his left hand, reaching out to help Iris up with his right. “Hold on\!” 

Iris reluctantly clasped her palm with Salem's icy cold hands and with a firm heave, Salem pulled Iris toward him, allowing her blaster to come parallel to his rifle, creating a perfect, calculated aim upon the serpent. Iris felt her balance become suddenly steady and exhaled deeply, firmly gripping her blaster with her Makobreaker readily loaded.

“Now\!” Salem exclaimed, pulling the trigger on his sniper rifle. His Tenasher slug sped through the sky, widening its jaw and chomping viciously through the air. Its scales reacted violently, hardening up and shadowing the light that once reflected upon it.  Iris didn't hesitate either, immediately reacting to Salem's motion and releasing her new Makobreaker slug. As it plunged forwards towards the serpent, its red fins stretched backwards, gliding between the currents of the winds and speeding up. Two massive jaws were pursuing the Rainbow Serpent that lunged forwards, chomping on its rainbow scales. The Serpent screeched and suddenly squirmed backwards, flinging both slugs away with one big flick. Its sounds wailed in pain, wrangling around the lakebed and finally burrowing back into its water hole deep beneath the lake's surface. 

Iris and Salem rushed forwards, approaching the water's edge on where the serpent had burrowed. At the two natural embossed tunnels that burrowed into the caverns, a silhouette and slug appeared quietly just out of their sight. They weren't clear, rather quite blurred by the shadows, blending in nicely to mask their identity. The slug however, jumped into a position where the light above perfectly lit its shape up, revealing itself as a modified Hexlet. The Vexlet gazed ominously, perplexed at Iris and Salem's presence at it mediated at the fall's ledge. The scrambling sound of water rushing downwards masked its screeches, then it disappeared. Its crimson body radiated a strange menacing aura as it hopped skittishly away, disappearing into the shadows of the jungle. 

“What is…that?” Iris began, her words jumbling into a concerned expression.

“It's a Hexlet…But different…” Salem began. “It's…darkened somehow…It's almost like it's a *ghouled* version of itself…”

“Like a Vexlet? I've heard stories of dark water…something that is able to contaminate all living beings…It changes them into a more chaotic and spasmodic version of itself…” Iris explained, her mind racing with the possibilities that it meant for them moving forward.

Salem sighed, slinging his sniper back onto his back. After a long exhale, his voice sharpened, gazing towards the fall's ledge. “Whatever it is, the Hexlet is a super-rare, one of a kind slug known to the jungle. We need to find it. Now.”

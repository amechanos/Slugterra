### Legend of the Drunken Devil, Part 2

The air in Clinkton was thick with the smell of ozone and spent blaster charges. Tens of mecha-beasts rolled into view upon the sand dunes, now slowly trotting towards the quiet town of Clinkton. The wanted fugitive blasted furiously within the town, still in their rampage. Slingers from Steamvale surrounded the scene like deadly hunters, waiting for the right moment. Gangs eyed the golden prize, their engines grunting a predatory roar. It was an all for nothing stunt where any false move would turn this into a bloodbath.

“What is your plan exactly?” Nole whimpered, unsure of Nash's intent. They had been blindly following Salem's trail, a faint path of footprints of his mech etched into the burning sand.

“Dreamise,” Nash smiled, pointing to the beige slug strapped onto his belt. “This fella can do god's work\!” 

Nole looked worried, expressing concern about Nash's plan. But still, the concern was still heavily skewed on the situation at hand. As they closed in on Clinkton, the view of the girl arrogantly destroying the town became clearer. The roars of slugs being fired continued to bellow through the town, obediently returning to Iris after their destruction. As Nash reached the outer ring of town, other hunters had begun to gather nearby. There was no denying that the static in the air drew low, now clear as ever before, twitching on each and everyone’s skin. The town was a firing range. From the elevated ridge of the outer ring, the destruction took on a rhythmic, terrifying beauty. **Iris**, glued at the center of the cratered town square rampaged on, her silhouette framed by the glowing trails of her flying slugs. They streaked through the air like miniature comets, searing the surroundings and fogging the town up. It was almost impossible to see through the mess. A few bold and arrogant slingers rushed in head first, holding their blasters up, ready to fight. But it was no use. Moments later grunts echoed about, the slingers falling back first, off their mechs into the scorching sand. Bodies toppled and mechs erupted, while blood began to trail and sweat evident in their breaths. Nobody dared to move.

“How do you expect to hit her with the Dreamiese?” Nole whispered, trying not to spoil the plan. The rare Dreamiese wasn’t a common slug around the desert, and it was rather powerful to subdue targets. For Nash, it was his ticket to secure Salem’s friend in the safest way possible. He looked at the little slug with a glimmer of hope. The Dreamiese slowly looked up and nodded, signalling its ability.

“I don’t,” Nash sighed, clutching the Dreamiese’s shell in his hand tightly. “As a matter of fact, I don’t even know where Salem is. The best shot we have is when the dust clears…But who knows when it’ll be gone…”

Nole gazed at the view carefully, trying to make out exactly what was going on. “But she might be gone when it clears\!”

Suddenly, a loud crunch devastated the area, silencing the rampage that had been going on. It was so unexpected that Nash almost lost his balance, scaring the daylights out of Nole as well. It felt unreal, like a monster had just eaten the tension, removing everything uneven and dusty about the atmosphere. “What just happened?”

A loud shout disrupted the silence that had just engulfed Clinkton. It sounded like a boy, corrupted by revenge. The dust had completely disappeared as the boy faded into view. The relentless pursuit of desperation fueled his motives, marching forwards with no intent of going back. Nash peeped back out, only to see him. Salem, acting like a maniac had stepped foot into the town, ignoring the patrol of the surrounding slingers. He was the one in a million, brave enough to approach Iris. Salem shielded his eyes, slowly pushing through the dust and debris. 

“Look at that, eh mate?” a slinger snickered, chuckling at Salem's audacity to challenge the Drunken Devil. “Is he stupid or something?”

“Iris…” Salem muttered, clutching his blaster now with his Menasher loaded in. It was his last hope. He didn't want to do it. “Don't make me do this…”

Salem’s Menasher, already in his blaster satiated over Iris’s corruption. 

“You broke the mask?” Iris snapped, noticing Salem’s heroic efforts of attempting to stop her. “And that’s my hoverbug\!”

“The dust?” Salem boasted. “Easy work for this ghoul, wouldn’t you say?”

The Menasher screeched again, louder than before. The sound was so piercing even Nash and Nole, quite far from the commotion, had to cover their ears. It became evident to Iris that the Menasher was much stronger than her own slugs. The Menasher was terrifyingly energetic, as if it wanted to bite her head off. No, it wasn't the same vibrant crocodile slug she had seen before— it had become a killer. For the first time since Iris had escaped the jungle, she felt frightened. Even at her strongest form, fused with the dark water.

Her body twitched, causing her limbs to almost spasm as her expression lunged forward, trying to catch Salem off guard. Salem didn’t flinch. Something had triggered inside of him. Her Hoverbug that once stood beside her, tucked itself into one of Salem’s shells, watching the dire scene unfold right before its eyes.

“What do you want?” Iris hissed, loading her petrified Makobreaker. Both blasters aimed at one another, waiting for one of them to pull the trigger. Dead silence shadowed the scene, the destruction finally calming down for once. The slingers watched in awe, freezing in their tracks. The circular pattern of slingers surrounding the town had almost formed like a stadium of some sort, adding to the already heavy emotional weight Salem was carrying.

“I can fix you…Help you…” Salem muttered, his hand shaking. His blaster trembled, but his Menasher growled stably, readying its mega bite again.

“The Dreamiese must work, Nash\!” Nole whispered, spying on Iris and Salem's duel. The standoff was too climactic, neither of them daring to pull the trigger.

“I know,” Nash whispered, his grip tensing. Hunters eyed the prize. “Patience…”

Nole clung to the blasted rock for cover as Nash stood tall, loading his blaster. For a moment, it felt like Salem was going to pull the trigger, but the longer Nash watched, the more tense the duel got. Nobody dared to do anything. The surrounding slingers also watched eagerly as if it was a movie, identifying the perfect moment to capture the Drunken Devil for themselves. Either way, every slinger knew that sooner or later, something must happen. The duel wasn’t going to last long. 

A dry gust of desert heat howled between them, dragging a short zephyr that rustled Nash’s hair. It continued, sweeping up sand that ran across the thin, cracked ground as not a single muscle moved from him nor the crowd. Nothing was happening. Nobody was reacting.

Salem was *waiting*.

Nash did too.

Nole swallowed hard. “Why isn’t he shooting…?” he muttered, barely audible.

Nash exhaled slowly, eyes darting around. “No…”

A gang of slingers to Nash’s right motioned for action. Then a group on their left took off. Plans had begun to be put out, and Nash was behind. Nole scrambled around, panicking. Nash calculated for a moment, then strategised the plan. He knew it was rushed and badly thought out, but if they had to execute something, they had to do it now. The silence had already been masking the hidden steps to the bounty’s capture. Then, with a flick of his wrist, Nash signalled for action. 

“I need you to get in there and get Salem out,” Nash explained. “Tunnel beneath, nobody will see you. I don't want this slug to hit him…”

Nole nodded quietly, immediately beginning to tunnel beneath the commotion to reach Salem.

His paws dug at the Earth, carving his entrance as he burrowed his body deep inside, swiftly moving forward at a rhythmic pace. Each scoop, each sniff for direction grew close as Nole understood his job. Nash depended on him. Overhead, Nash aimed down sights, readying his shot the moment Nole had popped out. He could hear other slingers duelling, fighting for the bounty already. Slugs were flung in all directions but still, Iris and Salem stood completely still without a single movement or action. The vibrations that shredded the ground, only delaying Nole's entrance. But even safe underground, Nole could feel the growing pressure. The blasts rolled through the earth in deep, boneless waves, warping the tunnel walls, sending thin cascades of sand hissing down from the ceiling in ribbons. Each impact narrowed the passage a little more. Nole pushed harder, his elbows wide, knees driving forwards but the ground was beginning to answer back. Sand leaked through the seams of his tunnel in steady threads, packing against his shoulders, filling the space behind his heels. 

“Eek\!” Nole shrieked, the panic inside him only intensifying. He angled upward.

The resistance changed. The earth grew lighter, drier — almost crumbling against his palms now. He needed air. Nash fiddled uncontrollably, desperate for Nole to come in with the assist. It was as tense as it could get. Nole’s fist erupted like a call for help, finally dragging himself out of the tunnel and surfacing into the town. Light flooded in as he hauled himself out of the ground and everything was off.

Silence.

Salem stood several metres to his right, turning to notice Nole’s dramatic entrance. All the slingers including Iris herself took notice, freezing in motion as the tension broke, silencing the chaos. Nash couldn't help but facepalm, but realised the golden opportunity— His finger pulled back on the trigger faster than ever before, firing the Dreamiese before Salem could, interrupting the frozen duel. 

It sped towards Iris for a direct hit, striking her right in the body. She couldn’t react in time. Iris's head began to throb as the Dreamiese injected its effect, shutting her body down almost instantaneously. She limped for a moment, then her balance swung her body forwards and collapsed in front of her mech. Nole hurriedly ducked his head back into his tunnel, scurrying back to where he had come from.

The solo slingers, still waiting, knew what it meant the moment Iris fell in contact with the ground. They were too familiar with the dangerous and rare Dreamiese, capable of placing any target into a deep sleep for a prolonged time. She was taken out. It was anyone's bounty. It was a race. The sudden silence immediately jarred the surrounding tone, intensifying the already heightened stakes the rampage had set. Iris's violent energy that had been tearing the town apart had simply vanished. Salem stood frozen in the dust, his shield lowered, staring down at the Drunken Devil who was now just a pale girl asleep in the sand.  

“Iris…” his voice sounded like the song again, the one he heard when he entered the desert. Hypnotic and seductive, yet an illusion that was his own words. ​The moment of peace didn't last. His voice wasn’t the only thing that existed. Roars faded in as Salem was still planted in the ground, motionless. Engines began to vibrate through the ground as the slingers broke formation, rapidly closing the distance. They had been waiting for the right moment, and this was it. Nash pressed the accelerator on his mech down hard, racing towards the group. They needed to escape.

​"Salem, we need to go\!" Nash shouted, his voice snapping through Salem's shock. Nole was in the crossfire, already back searching for his mech that was engulfed by the sea of slingers. Galloping legs forcefully kicked outwards, creating deathly streets if anyone was to cross. Slingers fell as they pushed and slung slugs at each other, creating the ultimate conflict. It was chaotic, uncontrolled— like a prison brawl.

### Beyond the Wall

Under the sparkling Pacific laid the archaic and temporal Jungle. Secluded and hidden by the boundless barrier wall, the rainbow decorated, flora lush environment of the Jungle conveyed more than just greenery; it was a place where time felt different. Time felt dilated to an extent, where each step forward felt weightless. The jungle was like its own planet, being able to provide sufficient resources for the Lawa tribe who had lived there. Lakes shimmered, but never reflective of the environment, cascading the horizon into shifting glass. It felt like living in a perfect fantasy, only enclosed by the Boundless Barrier Wall. Even so, cracks of toughened spirits formed through the charming landscape.

“What's the hold up?” Salem asked as he laid within the canopies of the winding jungle trees. The tangled vines shielded against the soft Solaryte crystals above, casting a soft warm ray of light onto Salem's chest. His rifle sat quietly aimed towards the ground, his hands on the trigger. 

“You're taken off,” the voice boomed through the earpiece. “The target's location has changed,”

“Whatever,” Salem sighed, adjusting his glasses. His white hair, rustled up and wavy drooped down as Salem finally sat up, sliding his rifle back onto his back. “Break time\!”

Salem dusted the leaves off his lavender half-zip as he happily began his way back to Lahos Town. Salem padded his collar upright, cheerfully embracing his recent approval of joining the Lawa Hunting Division by the clean engraving of the word hunting on his collar. The serenity of the perching creatures sang softly, adding to the calming atmosphere. Each step welcomed a fresh new breeze of the cool jungle mint, crisp and cold to where it tasted like sparkling spring water. 

“New signal near temple, copy unit 27: Report back,” the voice chirped in Salem's ear again, alerting him. It disrupted the natural flow and his motion, causing him to pause for a moment and check his surroundings. He didn't notice his close proximity towards the Temple of the Lawa, a sacred burial spot of the tribe's first leader. What was the signal doing here? What could have triggered it? Salem grew curious, beginning to look for potential triggers.

“Nothing. Probably static,” Salem replied, holding the button on the earpiece to clearly communicate his findings. He didn't see anything out of place or unusual, with the flora identical to that of moments ago. The greenery was motionless and the temple was intact, untouched. Salem traced the slimy moss that had begun to grow along the crevices of the stone bricks that acted as the temple's foundation, feeling the ancient architecture for the first time of his life. The line went silent as Salem climbed the steps towards the entrance, taking a closer look. Suddenly, a scream echoed faint through the peculiar entrance guarded by Hexlet statues, mongering fear and drawing misfortune to those who were brave enough to disrespect the First Tribe Leader's tomb. Salem was too curious to not question the oddity, especially the high-pitched voice. Though faint, Salem intrinsically believed somebody had wandered into the temple by accident, breaking tradition. It wasn't Salem's duty to find whoever entered, but it felt wrong leaving whoever was in there alone to rot in the depths as he had heard stories of the Temple being an unforgiving place, charmed by the infamous bad luck slug, the Hexlet. 

“This is unit 27— reporting again on a signal near the temple…” Salem exclaimed, only for the line to be cut by a quick stutter then radio silence. The connection was weak and the technology wasn't developed enough. Salem only grew in guilt and worry every second he thought about the voice. It sounded weak and scared to where Salem felt like there had to be a justifiable reason to enter the sacred ground. Making sure nobody was around, Salem hurriedly crafted a simple torch with a bundle of sticks tied tightly together with the extra rope he carried and bolted in. The Temple's entrance stood grand as Salem held the wooden torch firmly, lighting his way forward. The steps were drenched with the moisture build up from the cavern ceiling seeping through the stone, creating a slimy and slippery surface that Salem had to carefully navigate. The deeper he explored, the more spooky it got. A pungent scent of damp moss struck Salem hard, followed by a bitter taste of limestone reaching the back of his tongue. As Salem pushed further into the bowels of the temple, the air grew heavy, thick with a static charge that made his skin prickle. The grand architecture gave way to narrow, jagged arteries of stone where the only sound was the rhythmic drip-tap of mineral-rich water. In the oppressive silence, the atmosphere shifted from mere antiquity to a cold, predatory stillness. Salem didn't flinch; instead, a strange numbness washed over him, pulling his mind into a hazy, daydreaming state where the history of his people felt written in the very oxygen he breathed. 

The gentle humming walls echoed stories of the past through the deep engravings of stories on the bricks. Salem continued quietly, embracing the effervescent sensation that the rippling moisture had sparked. 

Drip drop.

Salem could feel the wetness, coldness and stiffness of the passage forward. As he turned the corner, his fingers trailed along a chain of words embedded deep within the stone. 

*“jalan ke depan berbahaya…”*

Then it appeared. Just ahead, a tunnel appeared on the right with a bright green, luminescent river stretching through the center of it . It was the perfect timing as his torch was burning out, almost diminishing as he reached the tunnel.

“Well, hello there Glowbyss\!” Salem smiled happily, wading through the water. Salem was quite knowledgeable of the environment and how the water was merely an illusion crafted by the sneaky Glowbyss slugs. Glowbyss slugs were known for their bright, dazzling lights being able to blind enemies or to light up areas in a neat neon green glow. As Salem bent down, he plucked the green light out. It was like a domino effect as once one Glowbyss was removed from the chain, the others periodically switched their lights off, creating the effect that the lights within the tunnel slowly shut down. The Glowbyss slug, now dormant, stood lightless on his palm, shining a new path forward. Salem carelessly threw his now burnt out torch aside, walking forward in the cold waters. As the Glowbyss slugs fully darkened the tunnel, there was a loud hiss from the other end of the tunnel where Salem suddenly turned to. Salem's fist was able to mask the light of the Glowbyss he carried completely to where it didn't expose his position. He quickly repositioned in the tunnels, attempting not to draw the other person or entity's attention. 

“Who's there?” Salem whispered sharply, expecting an answer from the shadows. His voice however was quite muffled by his fear, thinking it was a strange beast or monster lurking in the temple. Whatever it was, he needed to be careful. But the water still splashed. The Glowbyss now reacting to Salem's tight grip suddenly flashed, struggling as his fingers tightened around its body. Salem didn’t intend to hurt him, but seeing the Glowbyss signalling for the other Glowbyss to turn back on, he knew he was in trouble. The lights within the tunnel were reignited by the luminescent river, now back to its full glow.

“Who are you?” the silhouette suddenly exclaimed as they appeared in front of Salem who finally peaked around the corner.. It was a girl who looked exhausted and terrified. Her voice shook and her grip was unstable while the Armashelt that was loaded whimpered, also frightened. 

“Calm down\!” Salem exclaimed, reaching for his blaster in case the girl didn't hesitate to shoot. His hand graced by the lightness of his sniper quickly slung it overhead, loading a slug from his waist rack.  “I wouldn't draw blasters if I were you…Let's just say, you're too old fashioned…” 

“Try me you monster\!” the girl yelled, slinging her Armashelt straight towards Salem. Salem didn't hesitate to defend himself, slinging his prized Gyrophyll into action with the flick of a finger.

As the Armashelt lunged towards the Gyrophyll, it struck its claw. The Gyrophyll quickly hurled up into a ball-like shape right before the claw's impact, where it used the strike to generate an insanely high rotational speed, instantly propelling itself in a strange trajectory with the momentum the Armashelt had given it. The Gyrophyll, though knocked backwards, spun like a beyblade, rerouting back forwards and ripping through the air, knocking the Armashelt out instantly. 

“Time's up\!” Salem called as the Gyrophyll slowed down, returning to its protoform moments before colliding with the girl. Rather it was all too sudden as the girl stood motionless. Salem grew worried by the second, slowly approaching her.

But still, within that moment, perhaps it was still the immense build of heat spiralling into chaos within her body, pulsating her heart to unprecedented speeds, the girl slumped against the wall and passed out cold. Her feet dangled into the quiet stream. 

And again, there was silence. 

Salem, suddenly tripping into a state of concern, paced his movements, hoping she wasn't critically injured. He was certain that she was probably tired or even worse, dehydrated. Her pulse was slow but steady. No vitals were damaged, but the girl was already in deep slumber. Was it the duel? Salem was shocked. Nevertheless, the hoverbug was scared and worried. Sighing in guilt and shame, Salem carefully carried the girl out of the temple. She didn't seem to be of the Lawa tribe, as if there was land beyond the wall. Were the legends told by the elders true? Salem was confused, accepting this truth in an awkward way. He was excited, but also terrified of what was truly beyond the wall. With the girl cradled carefully in his arms, Salem traced his movements backwards, heading back from where he came from and sprinting out of the temple’s exit. His mind raced faster than his feet. All he could think of was asking questions to this potential foreign person when they awoke.

“Where am I…?” the girl grumbled as she finally awoke hours later. Her eyes buzzed open, slowly adjusting to the soft, velvety lights that shimmered like bokeh flakes.

“My name is Salem Kahue, sorry if I startled you back in the Temple of the Lawa before. Who are you? I haven't seen you around here before…”

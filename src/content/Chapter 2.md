### Fossil Worms

The mecha's uneven trotting against the dunes echoed in Iris's ears as they ventured into the vast expanse the region had to offer. Each step forward vibrated the dense sand. Silhouettes of creatures ravaged in the background, flailing against the scorching crystals that beamed from above.

Amongst the Bomb Flats, Grenukes dangled quietly from the hanging stalactites on the cavern ceiling. A tapestry of muddy colours eroded the environment, complimenting the jagged rocky floor that poked out in all directions. The sound of the mecha's legs, now against the solid, hard rock only grew louder. 

Again and again. 

Then, there was a growing tick that rang in Iris's ear. It didn't stop, it sounded like something was burrowing nearby, shaking the ground. 

“Fossil Worms…Huh,” Iris questioned, reading the smart display her mech had shown of the nearby region. The small, compact device attached to the mech was perfect, providing real-time information as Iris travelled. “Quiet, leech-like creatures that dwell in the sand…I better be careful…”

Now finally along the edge of the Bomb Flats, Iris began to ride along the edge, horrified by the fact a Grenuke from one of the hovering stalactites would fall. Slowly pacing the area like a lost tourist, Iris was slightly displaced on where to go. She wasn't too familiar with the technology, especially the ones integrated onto the mecha. 

“What do you think, Fin?” Iris motioned, pointing towards a direction. Fin nodded in approval, suggesting the idea of exploration was one of the only ways Iris could find the wall. It also signalled to Iris that Fin wasn't familiar with the wall either, with it being such a mysterious entity embedded into the desert. 

Then there it was again. 

It wasn't the periodic splash of sand that crashed like a wave, but a much more disturbing swallow of sand followed by a high pressure bolt of air shooting it back out like a geyser. It felt much closer and Iris could feel the particles of sand tickle her skin, indicating something was coming.

“Is that…a Fossil Worm?” Iris asked, expecting an answer from Fin that just watched the sand scatter back into the desert. The two, basically motionless, were terrified and curious. “We should be safe…As long as we’re on the rocks…right?” 

Fin nodded unsurely, tracing the worm’s movement with his eye. Iris gently pushed the accelerate handle and boosted forward, continuing on with the trek to the wall. She wanted to ignore the fact a large beast was travelling parallel to them, but Fin was almost always eyeing the moving sand signalling to Iris that the beast shouldn’t go unnoticed. 

Almost with warning, a huge tsunami of sand spewed out from the ground, creating a fog of sand that blurred vision and polluted air. Iris, covered in dust and coughing manically from the inhaled sand was forced to hit the brakes, stopping randomly within the cloud of sand. It gave the worm ample time to strike and before the cloud could finally diffuse, it had burrowed a large hole, ready to emerge from the underground.

The leech-like creature, stitched from shiny scales, all majestic in its deep orange body had begun to surface. It didn't seem to have eyes, no, rather it seemed to target the sound of its prey, now almost halfway out of its burrow, looming over the rocky region. Poking out of the sand, its motion upwards revealed its vampire drawn mouth, coloured in a deep stench of blood and dirt. Each segment of its body, distinguished by a gleaming metallic piece of armour that resembled a shell flexed, bending in motion to create the terror of the sands. 

Iris and Fin watched in amazement and terror, and now with the sand gone, Iris regained control of her mech. The Fossil Worm leant in to slam the rocky surface with its body, attempting to hit Iris but Iris quickly dodged, deciding to run. She pressed forward, trying to escape. The Fossil Worm however had no plans to leave, recognising the roar of the mecha’s engine. It screeched a loud wail, communicating its anger and commencing its pursuit.

The Fossil Worm suddenly erupted like a dragon, spiralling towards one of the stalactites that held Grenukes. Its powerful body slam against the hanging piece of rock instantly snapped it off the ceiling. The Grenukes one by one tumbled off the surface, falling like fragile glass shards at maximum velocity. Reaching their terminal speeds of 160km/h, the Grenukes slowly velocimorphed, changing into deadly ticking bombs awaiting to detonate.

Iris shook her head in panic as she forcefully pushed the acceleration handle even harder. It was run or fight. She didn't stand a chance against the creature with just a hoverbug. The Fossil Worm rattled like a snake, burrowing again underground to sprint parallel to Iris. 

The Fossil Worm had the easier path to take, a straight beeline forward while Iris had to carefully weave through the minefield of Grenukes that were ticking away, ready to detonate. But right as the first Grenuke detonated, a chain reaction, falling like dominoes occurred, exploding each Grenuke one by one, barely catching Iris. Suddenly, as Iris jeered right, she felt her mech's legs collide with one of the Grenukes that hadn't detonated yet. With a loud fizzling sizzle, the Grenuke exploded, sending Iris flying off her mech onto the ground. There was still quite some distance until the Bomb Flats finally had ended, marking the safety zone. Iris crawled weakly towards her mech, carefully avoiding the flying debris and explosive Grenukes. The smoke and dust was terrifyingly high, forcing Iris to cover both her eyes and mouth in order not to inhale the cough prone dust and toxic fumes felt. As she moved blindly, she finally felt the warm metallic legs of her mech, still geared up and ready to run. Uncovering her eyes and gasping for breath, Iris quickly saddled up and accelerated forwards, ignoring the danger of a few more Grenukes, lying still dormant stuck in the ground, not yet exploded. Her mech stood strong, leaping over the tactical bomb planted on the rocky surface with a flurry with debris and flame erupting behind her. It felt straight out of a movie as she landed safely, a miracle to still be alive after the traumatic experience. Her eyes quickly darted backwards, seeing the aftermath of the Grenuke scattering. There was a huge crater now within the once smooth Bomb Flats, thanks to the terrifying Fossil Worm. 

Then she heard it again. A loud gurgle from the sand. 

The Fossil Worm was still close. Alive and unharmed. She had to keep running. 

It was her only option. 

*“No way I can fight that thing\!”* Iris thought to herself again.

The Fossil Worm only grew more energized the more she ran, now leaping out of the air and diving into the sand like it was water. Each dive, each splash dusted sand towards Iris and her mech, almost jamming the engine.

“C’mon\!” Iris panicked, squeezing the acceleration handle even faster. The mecha’s speed was no match for the worm’s exponential speed. Iris could only think of swerving deeper into the more rocky area of the Bomb Flats, but as she prepared to turn, the terrain began to shift. It wasn’t flat land anymore.

As her mech bolted forwards, spine-like stems of rock appeared ahead, as if they were bursting out from the ground. It formed a jaw-like pattern, creating almost a maze-like territory. Iris kept momentum, speeding forward and weaving through the veins of rocks. Each action of dodging the rock structures helped her gain depth, slowly moving away from the edge. 

“The Vein Rocks: A secluded space with towering pillars of rock like stalagmites forming into an intricate jaw-like pattern. The area inhabits slugs mainly of the rock family including Ramstones, Armashelts and Crystalyds\!” a robotic voice played as Iris kept driving. 

“The Vein Rocks huh?” Iris read as she gazed at the newly updated display. As she continued forwards, Ramstones peeped out from the crevices of the rocks in which they called home, watching the Fossil Worm pursue Iris. “So glad for an AI to read me the information…”

But then, it hit her. With the help of the advanced technology present on the mech, her eyes flickered at the spark of a blue creature instead of a red Ramstone that resided in the Vein Rocks. No, it wasn’t the small blue horns of the Ramstones, it was a light, sky blue coloured creature coated with a thick gold armour. She had seen the hope that was needed to defeat the Fossil Worm.

Forward they went, the Fossil Worm was directly beside her ready to strike. The rock structures had finally grown less clumped together, creating a denser space. Iris was ready to finally escape until a final wave of sand was directed towards her, almost blowing her off balance. But this time behind the cloud of dust, the Fossil Worm leant in close, denying Iris from escape. As she moved forward through the fog, the Fossil Worm was already ahead and had prepared for another body slam. Iris couldn’t react fast enough, slamming the breaks but still slamming into the worm’s body, flinging herself forwards and off her mech. The Fossil Worm flinched, jumping backwards at the heavy slam from the mecha onto its body. 

Its gnarly breath lingered in the surrounding air as it stood dominant, ready to deal the final blow to Iris, who was now weakened and separated from her mech. Iris had tumbled along the rocky surface, finally rolling beside one of the many jaw-like rock structures. Before Iris could get a grip on the situation, where her mech had also toppled over, the sound of a roaring pain emitted from the Fossil Worm. As she turned around to check her surroundings and where her mech went, she was frighteningly welcomed by the blood-thirsty Fossil Worm bathing beneath the scorching golden light of the desert, looming over her as if she was its next meal. More of the gruesome stench of blood came from its rotted mouth, preparing to strike Iris any second.

Iris backed up, only to feel two towering rock structures obstructing her path of escape. It felt like it was over, but there was still hope, to where a gentle nudge from a strange silhouette alerted Iris there was a way to defeat this beast. Iris felt it on her right shoulder, where a strange feeling tingled through her golden aviator-like jacket. It was a shimmer of gold, perhaps the same rare Armashelt she had seen just a few minutes ago.

“Can you…take him down?” Iris asked, reaching for the Armashelt that stood on her shoulder. Still seated on the rocky surface, Iris could feel the vibrations below growing ever so slightly, signalling the Fossil Worm was ready to attack. It hissed loudly at Iris and the Armashelt. As she held out her hand, the Armashelt nodded courageously, arming itself into her blaster without any hesitation. “Well, here goes nothing\!”

The vital quick release flung the Armashelt out as Iris closed her eyes, while the Fossil Worm lunged without hesitation towards Iris. Iris prepared herself for the worst, looking away all terrified. For a second, there was silence. 

The vibrations paused.

Nothing but the sound of a heavy metal bullet echoed, almost nullifying the creepy desert gales. The Armashelt ripped through the sky as it hurled its thick plated armour at the Fossil Worm, striking first with a brutal uppercut, sending the Fossil Worm sky-high. It revealed the abnormal and colossal length of the worm, with its whole body in the air flapping around like a fish out of water. As it was flung, the Armashelt had prepared its next attack, now using both of its heavy claws to deal a devastating blow, knocking the Fossil Worm backwards into the sand. The Fossil Worm's saliva of a strange blue substance splattered outwards, barely missing Iris who sat still. All around her was blue coloured liquid, all gooey and disgusting. Its body laid still in the sand, basking underneath the light of the crystals above

Screeches from the worm echoed in short, periodic screams, quickly squirming back into the sand to where it had burrowed, to escape the effects of long-light exposure. Iris grunted as she used the rock structures to stand up, finally dusting the sand off her jacket. 

“You're a tough little one aren't ya? Do you want to join my team?” Iris smiled as she found her balance. The Armashelt, all tough and brave, grinned brightly, gleaming at the dazed Hoverbug that perched on Iris's left shoulder. Neither could say no, so the Armashelt happily joined Iris's arsenal, powering her skills as a slug slinger up.

“Hah\! If it weren't for you, I'd be worm food\!” Iris chuckled as she continued forward on her mech. Onwards she travelled, further west she went. She wanted to see the wall in all its beauty. She was getting close and could feel it from even the Vein Rocks.

Before she knew it, her fingers gently swept the surface of the display, scrolling the page of the Fossil Worm further down. She didn't know it was a touchscreen. And there it read. Fossil Worm's weakness? Armashelts and Crystalyds.

### The Temporal Jungle, Part 2

Iris ran back into the hut, avoiding contact with Ravik. The gatling gun Ravik had sat unused on his mech as he followed in pursuit, confronting Iris in the hut. Lahos town sat dead silent in the night. Ravik knocked first, then barged in, finding Iris stealing a vial that contained the special serum. Her fingers wrapped tightly around the vial filled with the yellow liquid as Ravik's footsteps trotted against the wooden floorboards. His figure stood looming at the doorstep, appearing almost like a demon preventing Iris’s escape.

“I know you’re behind this…” Iris muttered, removing the caps off of the vial with the serum inside. The scent of wild berries fused with a strange, metallic stench flooded the hut, causing Ravik to almost spit. The singular light source that dangled overhead hummed statically, only adding to the predetermined tension.

“No…” Ravik muttered oddly. He closed the distance, trying to disarm Iris of the serum. His steps were calculated, making sure not to further anger Iris. He needed Iris to side with him.

“Yeah?” Iris shouted. Ravik stared ominously as Iris held the serum in her hand. He could see Iris trembling with fear, her hands shaking and her balance uneven. Frightened by whatever was going to come, Iris had to make a decision. Ravik stepped closer. Then, without hesitation, Iris chugged the serum down. The sour taste choked down her mouth as she swallowed hard, almost gagging and spitting it out. But she resisted. The serum was injected into her system. 

The yellow fluid churned inside Iris's body as the chemicals reacted violently, causing Iris to lurch forwards. Ravik stood silent, expressed by a mix of disappointment and distress. The serum was never meant to be consumed as a liquid, it was supposed to be inhaled when the liquid turned into a gas. Moreover, Ravik’s concern was that the serum was never tested on humans, only slugs, not knowing what the effects would produce. He was terrified. Suddenly, a loud roar echoed from outside. It sounded like Aleku. He had overheard the commotion. Frustrated, his blazeful mood unleashed a Pyringo that flew through the hut’s door, targeted at Iris. Ravik caught the Pyringo’s motive, leaping out of the way and crashing onto the floor while Iris stood drunk from the serum, with the Pyringo inches away from burning the flesh off her face. The devilish charge of flame accelerated faster as its screeches grew louder, its own flames to only grow larger. The ignition felt dramatically close as Iris caught the Pyringo in her vision, ready to collide with her. Iris blinked, the slug slowing down in mid-air. It felt supernatural for a moment, but the Pyringo slowed down significantly. It was like a supernatural effect almost, but Iris couldn't tell what was happening. Had some external force slowed the Pyringo down? Or was it time that had slowed down itself? She stepped back and turned her head to avoid the Pyringo's inferno grasp, dodging it gracefully. The Pyringo's wrath didn't stop however, striking the hut instead and engulfing it with its devastating black flames. The vials were shattered, shards of glass falling like rain. Iris glided between each fragment as they fell slower than usual, avoiding any damage. The liquid quickly seeped into the ground as planks of wood that once fixed the hut up snapped, tumbling down onto the uneven ground.

The ruins of the archaic hut lay scattered. Aleku’s silhouette emerged from the rubble and fire, and approached Iris who was perfectly unharmed, just dazed and still standing amidst chaos. 

“Aleku…What has gotten into you?” Ravik grunted, pulling himself up from the debris. He patted off the dust and dirt off his vest as Aleku got closer to Iris. 

“They don’t belong here. The jungle rejects them— and so do I. What would you do if she escaped and killed everyone?” Aleku demanded, shouting as if he was the chief. Aleku's barbaric tone terrified Iris. No, Iris couldn't stay in the Jungle. She had no place— she was being hunted like an animal. She had entered someone else's territory. Aleku’s fist came rushing in, trying to hit Iris. Iris dodged again somehow, her movements almost being perfectly precise. “...and yet, you don’t fight back?” 

Iris’s eyes squinted in terror as she ducked again under Aleku’s fury, sprinting as far as she could. Ravik quickly took pursuit, following Iris who was trying to escape the glade. The dense canopy of trees helped camouflage herself, but Ravik didn’t lose sight. They were bolting through the shadows of the night. Iris ran for her life, out and away from the eyes of the tribe. She had to survive. 

At last, the trees finally cut off, leading to a small hill that housed a small cave entrance on its side. Iris didn’t think and threw herself in, hoping to hide away and escape for good. As Iris slid in, her boots hit the damp, slick floor of the cavern with a muffled thud. The air here was a heavy, oppressive weight that clung to her skin, carrying the sharp, mineral tang of wet limestone and the metallic taste of stagnant earth. 

The cavern was quite short in height, Iris barely being able to stand due to the abundant amount of the dangling overhead crystals. The visual noise surrounding her became an immediate, overwhelming assault. No, this cavern wasn’t ordinary. It didn’t have the conventional natural rock walls; instead it employed rare panels of silvered glass, creating a cold and artificial throat of endless reflections. Moreover, translucent crystals grew in jagged, crowded clusters from the floor and hung like wicked spears from the low ceiling, crowding her field of vision. The only source of light was from the dim, rhythmic, pink light that pulsed from the crystals, catching the razor-thin edges of the mirrors and splintering into a forest of sharp, glowing needles. Every direction she turned looked like a vast, shimmering cathedral where the distinction between solid wall and empty air had completely vanished.

“Iris?” a voice echoed. No, it wasn’t Salem’s. It was Ravik’s. Ravik had found the cavern. This was bad. With every tentative step Iris took, the mirrors punished her movement by duplicating her image a thousand times over. She watched herself move in fractured segments, trapped inside infinite crystal prisms that stretched endlessly into the dark. The ceiling dipped lower, the jagged spears of crystal forcing her to stoop and bringing the humming, glowing heat of the stones closer to her face. 

Iris whimpered in despair as she looked for any forms of slugs from the cavern that could assist her. It wasn’t obvious, but soon enough, Iris began to hear strange hums coming from the crevices between the crystals.  Amythistle slugs, with their soft lavender bodies, sang in high, angelic frequencies that reacted to the shifting pink light, while the Refracents danced like living mirrors, their hard shells tapping gently and rhythmically against the fragile crystals. The ever so rare Crystalyds also peeked occasionally, watching Iris navigate the labyrinth of her own reflection.

“I see you\!” Ravik’s voice growled inside Iris’s ear, his roars echoing infinitely in the sea of crystals. The Amythistles’ singing began to become a discordant cacophony that made Iris’s head throb with a dull, insistent ache. She felt a sickening wave of vertigo wash over her; the floor, though solid stone, now looked like a bottomless pit of luminescence, making her knees weak as she struggled to find a steady point of reference. Everything around her was either translucent, transparent, or maddeningly reflective. Then, a shimmer of red shot into Iris's eyes, catching the glimpse of a gatling gun aimed towards the cavern ceiling. Suddenly, an Omnithistle rushed between the crystals, smashing the overhead ones that began to fall in a domino chain down onto Iris.

Iris’s jaw dropped as she watched the crystals fall in time with another, thinking her time was up again. But it happened again. No, it wasn’t an illusion anymore. Iris knew this was not real. It felt like gravity had defied its laws as the crystals froze in time, barely moving down as she stood still gazing at the beauty of the magenta clumps that were freely solidified into space. She weaved through the crystals effortlessly, and slowly, the crystals finished their path by shattering as they collided on the ground. Then, Iris remembered, recalling what Salem had said.

*“...this reaction slows their perception of time…”*

Iris swiped a nearby Refracent who was caught in the chaos and quickly loaded it into her blaster. “Let's see what you can do\!” 

Amidst the flurry of falling crystals the Refracent's body shimmered against them, its reflective coating tightening. It flicked its tail, redirecting the crystals towards Ravik who hid amongst the crystals. The crystal tips dug deep, leaving sharp but shallow wounds on Ravik's skin. Ravik quickly retreated, carrying his gatling gun that was full of Omnithistles.

*“This girl is tougher than she looks…”* Ravik thought, underestimating Iris's skills. He had only wanted to bring her back to Lahos Town, where Aleku had other plans for her.

“Ravik\! Where are you?” Iris yelled, her voice ricocheting off the crystals. The constant ringing of echoes bounced within the cavern, adding to the already brainwashing singing of the Amythistles. Iris couldn't bear the rising pressure, again, getting to her head. To make matters worse, as she spun around trying to locate Ravik, the cavern toyed with Iris, completely flipping the script and presented Ravik's figure in a circular array, camouflaging him among the crystals. Amongst the reflections stood the true figure, standing a fair distance away, frowning in a disappointed expression.

“Let's see how you handle this…” Ravik grunted, holding the trigger on his gatling gun down. Omnithistles growled loudly in the barrels, launching one by one at ridiculous speeds. 8 lightning shaped crystals orbited each Omnithistle as they flew, preparing its lethal barrage. Strands of crystals flew towards Iris, slowing down again. Again, the crystals decelerated heavily, almost freezing in time as Iris slowly walked past the few shards. Her fingers grazed the static crystals, suddenly feeling the sharp pain. The crystal tore through her finger, blood spewing out. 

“Argh\!” Iris exclaimed, finally understanding the serum. The serum was exactly like bullet time from a movie Iris had watched back on the Burning World. Yes, she was just in slow motion. Everything else moved normally, her movements appearing instantaneous to others. “Tell me sooner,”

Iris focused, but her hands were shaky. She grasped her blaster firmly, aiming towards the incoming Omnithistles. Her breath hitched, exhaling stronger than she used to, but with reason. Her heartbeat spiked, her breath becoming yellow from the faint traces of the serum still stuck in her mouth. It wasn't much, but it was all she needed for her plan. “C'mon buddy, I'm counting on you\!” 

The Refracent leapt out as Iris pulled the trigger, entering the flow state. Though it wasn't travelling at the speed Salem had once told Iris, she thought it would still work— just as a slight boost of precision and accuracy. The Refracent homed in onto the Omnithistles, reading their shots as they finally fired their sharp, critical crystals. Iris held her hands, praying for it to work. It looked impossible, and probably it was, but the enhanced Refracent fought hard, easily proving its advantage over the fragile Omnithistles. The Refracent danced in the air, bouncing each shard on its tail and taking down the Omnithistles. Each needle like crystal needed nothing more than just a simple flick, easily deflecting the ruby red stones away.  Ravik stood unimpressed but terrified as four shards shot towards him, suddenly skewering the advantage. One shard pinned straight through his left hand, disarming Ravik of his gatling gun and the rest followed through, pinning Ravik directly against the uneven wall. He felt his back pressing against the cold crystals that could pierce his skin anytime.

“It's over, Ravik,” Iris demanded. 

“Over?” Ravik snorted. “You're making a big mistake…The jungle is restless, and *they* will force it to grow…You're nothing against Aleku or Lucian…They're much stronger than me…”

Iris snapped, her expression breaking and her mood shattered. She stomped on the gatling gun, breaking it almost instantly as she left Ravik alone, pinned eternally against the wall.  “No, I'll just come back stronger…”

With her new Refracent slug, Iris slowly made her way through Million Mirror Cavern, trying to find a way out. She was too busy finding her way to notice her veins slowly fading crimson red, her skin growing paler by the hour. It was subtle, almost unnoticeable where Iris ignored it. Then, a familiar warm breeze jousted against her. It seeped from the tunnel above her, almost as if it was another secret tunnel. 

*“No way…Is that…home? The desert…?”*

### Legend of the Dreaming Devil, Part 3

“What did you do to her?” Salem asked worriedly.

Nash, awkward but optimistic, grimaced at the situation. “My Dreamiese slug…”

The beige little slug waved joyfully at Salem who threw back a disfigured expression, unsure what to make of it. 

“This guy puts people in a deep sleep…Quite powerful for tasks like this…” Nash explained.

Salem snapped. “Why didn’t you tell me this earlier? This could’ve been settled so much easier— that ass of a duel made me feel so…uncomfortable\! Using my Menasher…It’s not me…” His voice winced, expressing his guilt. “How long does the sleep last?”

“The effect lasts approximately 3 days\!” Nole exclaimed cheerfully, breaking the chattering tension. Nole dragged Iris and her mech behind him, towing her with all his haste. There wasn’t a moment to waste. 

“Which ISN'T enough to get back to the Ridgelands\!” Nash rolled his eyes, forging up a new plan.

Nole snickered, having already predicted Nash's response. “I know a shortcut\!”

The group was already at a consistent pace, racing out of the town away from the other hunters. They had to leave. The desert swallowed the town behind them fast. Clinkton's glow faded into the sandy haze within minutes, replaced by the open, hostile expanse of the Drunken Desert. 

"She's heavy," Nole muttered, glancing at the tow cable straining behind him. Iris's mech dragged against the sand, its dead weight pulling his engine into a constant low whine.

"Stop complaining," Nash replied flatly, scanning the horizon behind them.

“Wait…” Salem interrupted, breaking the banter. “Where are the hunters? I swear I saw at least 50 before…”

“Hunters?” Nole exclaimed, scanning behind again. Nash was the only other who picked up the bizarre fact. Salem was right, no hunters were chasing after them. Had a battle occurred as they were leaving? Suddenly, a loud roar echoed from the almost faded town, a strange silhouette speeding closer. Something was coming. It was a hunter.

Nole slammed against the rusted dashboard of his mech, pushing the lever until the engine screamed in protest. "Who is that?\!"

Behind them, the silhouette had already jumped a massive distance, only several metres behind Nole who trailed at the back. A massive plume of ochre dust rose like a tidal wave, surfing towards the front of the group. A black-iron monstrosity built from the skeleton of a semi-truck, reminiscent of a Thylacoleo roared, racing forwards with weapons mounted on its body. Spikes of rebar protruded from its wheel wells, spinning like meat grinders. Nash clung to his mech, leaning ever more forwards. 

“What the hell?\!” Nash squealed, horrified by the beast that was following them. All he could hear was a loud grunt followed by a satanic snicker. Drifting along the dunes, his movement was slick, abusing the slopes to gain momentum, diving airborne for a heartbeat for a quick boost of speed. The sand from the impact scattered everywhere, fogging the surroundings. 

"No, no, no\! He wants *her* bounty\!" Salem yelled over the roar of the wind. The panic and concern was all that in his voice. He was terrified. "Which way do we go\!?"

A gout of flame erupted from the hunter’s twin exhaust stacks, closing a decent distance. The front grill, beaming out with a pair of devilled blood red eyes, loomed in Nole’s rearview mirror, filling the frame. He was the slowest of the group, already falling behind. 

Taking the glance again into his rearview mirror, all Nole could see in the shadowed face of the hunter was a diabolical streak that suggested he found the absolute carnage of the hunt to be the only fun thing in life. A mocking swagger, paired with the engine's sudden roar sounding like stones grinding in a blender jerked Nole back forwards, terrified to look back again. As the hunter rolled forward, easily matching Nole’s acceleration, bolded black letters spelling “KANE” lined the side of his mech as the hunter drew parallel with Nole’s mech. The hunter grunted, alerting Nole as he saw his hand reaching for the connector that towed Iris’s mech. 

“Drop the girl. I’ll make it quick.” the hunter grumbled. A turret rose from the back of his mech, arming his arsenal of Hoprocks that loaded the barrel beneath. 

“Eek\!” Nole shrieked, turning his mech dead right away from the hunter. Nash turned to see what the commotion was about and froze— the hunter was more than just a regular hunter. By the branding, he knew what they were up against. Kane leant forwards, showing his scarred muscle and scavenged Kevlar. His presence, anchored by an industrial respirator hissed like a desert viper as he exhaled deeply, his hands dropping low to try to disconnect the cable. He saw Nole’s terror, edging closer and closer as he adjusted his mech’s direction. 

Nash, worried, loaded his Rammstone and fired low, aiming at the ground ahead of Kane's mech. The Rammstone hit with a crack. A jagged wall of rock slammed up from the sand, spiking up to reach a solid chest height. Kane drove straight through it. The iron monstrosity didn't slow. It ate the rock wall, the front grill chewing through limestone like it was chalk. Debris sprayed sideways. A chunk clipped Salem's arm.

"That's one terrifying hull\!," Salem said, his voice flat. He stared at the mech with an awkward gaze.

“You don’t say\!” Nash clenched his jaw, trying to create more distance while helping Nole. “Can you try one of your ghouls? Maybe that’ll slow him down\!”

Before Salem could even reach for his blaster, a volley of Hoprocks from the turret blasted towards the terrain in front of the group, exposing them to the immense dust and explosions the Hoprocks began to create. A domino effect of concentrated blasts fired in their faces. Between the dust, an opening diluted the orange mess, Salem finally able to march forward without losing momentum. The minefield had slowed down for the moment, allowing him to clutch his blaster. He only had one shot. He didn’t want to lose himself. Ghouls were powerful, but it felt corrupt— vile and horrid, it felt like every time he used one, it took away some of his sanity.

“I got the turret\!” Salem exclaimed, firing his Pyrokill. It spun like nothing ever before, at speeds that transcended sound. The following volley of Hoprocks perfectly aimed at his Pyrokill, allowing it to reroute the explosive slugs straight at the turret. A chain of bangs lined the turret’s base, effectively pulling the turret out and ripping the core in half. The desert breeze swiftly eradicated its existence, carrying it quickly away before Kane could throw his anger.

“Nice\!”

A sign appeared, just a few hundred metres ahead. The sign labelled the land. 

*Mercury’s Bend \- 1km*  
*Tumblestone Cavern \- 4km*

“Aha\!” Nole exclaimed, pointing at the direction of Tumblestone Cavern. It was close, but they needed to keep their distance. “That’s it\! If I remember correctly, Tumblestone Cavern has a route back to the Ridgelands\!”

“Then that’s where we go\! Let’s hurry\!” Salem winced, motioning a sense of swiftness. 

The sand shifted colour, deepening into a rich, iron-red that seemed to drink in the glow of the solaryte crystals above. The light settled, staining the land in a dull, simmering warmth, as if the ground itself had begun to breathe heat. The mountains loomed closer, no longer distant silhouettes but towering bodies of layered stone. Bands of ochre, amber, and burnt umber ran across their faces like exposed veins. 

As they rounded the bend, the source revealed itself. The land simply gave way. A jagged chasm split the earth open without warning, its edges uneven and raw, tearing the path apart. Loose stones trickled constantly from the rim, bouncing once or twice before vanishing into the dark below. Pebbles trembled, then skittered loose. Watchful spires gazed like helpless lighthouses that organised the bend, reaching high but ultimately nowhere.

“This is Mercury’s bend?” Salem voiced, noticing the devastating drop down to a lower part of the desert. The path hooked hard right, overlooking the deep hole that seemed to lead to the abyss. It was dark with multiple spikes protruding out from the ground, reaching as far as the desert ceiling. Salem knew that if he was able to get Kane into the abyss, they were safe. “I have an idea…”

As Kane drifted around the bend, following hot on the group’s trail, Salem lost his sanity again, using his Quicksalvo to his advantage. “Eat this\!”

The Quicksalvo ripped out of the blaster at an alarming velocity, reaching megamorph as Its metallic shell stuck its magnetic tail out, gravitating all metallic objects within its radius to its toughened tail. It violently heaved Kane’s mech, brutally tossing him into the bend leaving his mech stuck on the slug’s tail. It seemed over. Nash and Nole cheered, but all Salem had to say was with a quiet sneer. Something had clicked.

\~

It didn’t take long for the group to arrive at Tumblestone Cavern, almost unscathed. They were almost home. At least that's what they thought. The air inside the cavern entrance was instantly thick with choking dust and the sharp scent of pulverized limestone. They trotted deep into the wider tunnels immediately, Nole navigating purely by the dim glow of bioluminescent moss clinging to the damp walls and his scent. They were almost there.  

Suddenly, the shadows shifted high on a rocky overhang directly above their path. There had been another path into the depths of Tumblestone Cavern, and it seemed impossible for a moment. Nole recalled the sign, unable to identify any routes. But Nash realised the truth first. Neither Nole or Salem had understood how deep they were now, already nearing the depths of Mercury’s bend. For Nash, he knew exactly who was here.

“I’ve played these games before…You’ve challenged the wrong guy,” the voice hissed. The darkness felt physical, a strong tug against the chain that connected Iris’s mech. 

“Argh\!” Nole screamed, trying to pull away from the mysterious grasp.

“It’s Kane\!” Salem yelled, assisting Nole to ensure Iris wasn’t stolen. Nash fired his Rammstone into the darkness, praying it hit Kane. Nothing happened. Suddenly, Kane moved, finally revealing himself from the shadows.

“You've screwed yourselves over innit?” Kane laughed.

Rocks began to tumble down as Kane had already leapt to the ledge, forcing the group to dodge quickly. Nash had sabotaged the group, cramping the space up even more. Their mechs quickly swerved around the corners, barely avoiding the boulders as they rolled forwards, crashing into the limestone walls and shattering into hundreds of pieces. Debris was launched, slicing the air like intricate daggers, scarring the skin of Nash.

“Ow\!” Nash screamed, pushing forwards through the pain. There was no stopping. Kane was still on their feet.

“No…How are you still alive?” Salem trembled, reaching for his ghouls again. Nash intrinsically swiped Salem’s blaster, already noticing his behaviour. 

Kane snorted, kneeling with a sinister smile. “I know every in and out of this drunken shithole,”

Nole nodded, playing along with Kane’s small talk. “Me too\!”

“You’ve got something that I want…” Kane grinned, slowly helping himself down. As he levelled himself with the group, an uneasy tension settled. Strangely enough, Kane didn’t draw blood, rather it felt like something was happening. “Look, I’ll make y’all a deal. How ‘bout, I get the girl and you lads get on your merry way, eh?”

Kane had grown closer, his appearance only getting clearer. The group stayed silent as Kane ruffled his messy beard, motioning his desire for Iris’s bounty. “Whadda ya say lads?”

Nole thought for a moment, then turned away. “No thank you, bye bye\!” 

Nole rushed off down the path, trying to catch Kane off guard. Nash and Salem couldn't comprehend Nole's decision, watching him sprint off on his mech with Iris down the tunnel. Hesitating for just a few moments too long, the two raced down with their mechs, only to be closely followed by Kane who was now enraged. 

“Oi\! Get back ‘ere ya cunts\!” Kane roared, running down the path with the disadvantage. 

The group scurried down the path, their mechs making easy work. The walls began to narrow, now forcing them to carefully squeeze their mechs through. Nole was sure they were close. Hope was around the corner.

“No\!” Nole exclaimed realising the path they took had led to a dead end. “It can’t be\!”

“Nole\!” Salem huffed, still racing down the winding path. Nole couldn't bear to process the information. Something was wrong. “Wait for us goddammit\!”

Nole recalled the path. He sniffed the dirt, verifying this was the path back to the Western Ridgelands. But it wasn’t. The route was sealed shut, encased by a smooth dirt wall that seemed natural to the terrain. They were cornered, and Nash could hear Kane’s stomps approaching extremely fast. Nole pressed his palm flat against the dirt wall. He'd misremembered, or the Ridgelands had shifted, or something had sealed it. Either way, it didn't matter.

"Nole," Salem said again, quieter this time.

"I know," Nole said. His voice had no shape to it. "I know."

“Found ya cunts\!” Kane laughed menacingly. He aimed his blaster at Nash’s chest, grinning from eye to eye. “The bounty is mine\!”

“No…You can’t take it…” Nash panted, trying to catch his breath. He held his arms out stoically, as if he was guarding the rest of the group. “Slugterra…needs…her…”

“Yer dead, mate\!” Kane exclaimed, pulling the trigger as Nash bravely protected the team. His exhale cut the tense sweating, silencing everything around him as he closed his eyes, ready to feel the pain of Kane’s Rammstone tear through his fragile body. Then, the sudden flash of cyan filtered through his eyes, blinding Nash and the group, only to find a muddied stem hanging from the ceiling as Nash reopened his eyes.

Welcome to the Western Ridgelands\!
